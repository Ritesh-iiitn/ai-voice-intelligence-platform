CREDIT PROTECTION & LIFE INSURANCE RIDERS POLICY

Document ID: DOC-INS-RIDER-2026 | Version: 1.4 | Effective Date: 2026-01-15

1. Rider Overview & Product Scope

The Credit Protection and Debt Cancellation Rider safeguards borrowers and their families against unexpected life events, involuntary job loss, and health emergencies during active loan tenors.

2. Protection Coverage Table

Rider Option Coverage Scope Maximum Benefit Waiting Period
Involuntary Unemployment Protection Monthly loan installment waiver upon certified redundancy or company shutdown Up to 6 monthly installments (max $3,000 / month) 60 days from policy inception
Total & Permanent Disability (TPD) Full outstanding principal payoff upon total permanent disability 100% of remaining loan balance up to $75,000 Immediate upon certification
Credit Life Insurance Settles entire remaining loan balance and distributes excess benefit to designated beneficiary Principal balance + $10,000 direct family benefit Immediate coverage
Multi-Vehicle Household Bundle Bundled credit protection across 2 or more household auto finance contracts 15% discount on combined protection premiums 30 days

3. Premium Calculation, Grace Period & Policy Lapse Rules

Monthly Premium: 0.35% per annum calculated on the declining outstanding principal balance, billed alongside the monthly loan installment.

Grace Period: Borrowers are granted a strict 30-day grace period for unpaid premiums before policy status enters default.

Policy Lapse: If a premium remains delinquent beyond 30 days, coverage officially lapses. Reinstatement is permitted within 60 days following full payment of arrears and submission of a good-health declaration.

Designation of Beneficiary: Borrowers may designate one or more primary and contingent beneficiaries. If no beneficiary is designated, benefits default to the borrower's legal estate.

Bank Referral Partner: Underwritten in partnership with Metropolitan Commercial Bank and affiliated regional banking partners.
