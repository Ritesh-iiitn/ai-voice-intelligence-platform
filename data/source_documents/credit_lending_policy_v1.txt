1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------------------
The Consumer Credit facility provides standard personal credit to retail borrowers.

1.1 General Eligibility Requirements:
- Age: Applicant must be between 23 and 60 years old at maturity.
- Citizenship / Residency: Permanent residents or legal citizens.
- Minimum Monthly Net Income: $3,000 per month.
- Minimum Credit Score: 680.

1.2 Credit Score Tiers & APR Schedules:
- Tier 1 (Score 750+): Fixed APR of 7.99% p.a.
- Tier 2 (Score 680 - 749): Fixed APR of 10.50% p.a.

2. LOAN TERMS AND FEES
----------------------
2.1 Loan Amounts:
- Minimum Facility Size: $5,000.
- Maximum Facility Size: $30,000.

2.2 Repayment Tenors:
- Available terms: 12, 24, 36, and 48 months.

2.3 Fees and Penalties:
- One-Time Processing Fee: 2.0% of approved principal.
- Prepayment Fee: 1.0% penalty charged if loan is settled within the first 12 months.
