1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------------------
The Consumer & Vehicle Flexi-Credit facility provides secured auto loans and unsecured personal credit to eligible retail borrowers.

1.1 General Eligibility Requirements:
- Age: Applicant must be between 21 and 65 years old at the time of maturity.
- Citizenship / Residency: Permanent residents or legal citizens with valid government-issued photo identification.
- Minimum Monthly Net Income: $2,500 per month (or equivalent in local currency).
- Employment: Minimum 6 months continuous employment at current employer, or 2 years verifiable self-employment income.
- Minimum Credit Score: 650 (Standard Underwriting Score).

1.2 Credit Score Tiers & APR Schedules:
- Tier 1 (Excellent, Score 750+): Fixed APR of 6.49% p.a.
- Tier 2 (Good, Score 700 - 749): Fixed APR of 8.99% p.a.
- Tier 3 (Standard, Score 650 - 699): Fixed APR of 11.75% p.a.
- Sub-650 Underwriting: Strictly subject to Exception Committee approval (Section 4).

2. LOAN TERMS, AMOUNTS, AND DISBURSEMENT
----------------------------------------
2.1 Loan Amounts:
- Minimum Facility Size: $5,000.
- Maximum Facility Size: $75,000 for vehicle loans; $35,000 for unsecured personal loans.

2.2 Repayment Tenors:
- Available terms: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, and 72 months.

2.3 Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limits:
- Standard maximum DTI ratio is 45% (inclusive of all revolving credit, mortgage, and proposed loan installment).
- If DTI exceeds 45%, the application must be declined or loan amount reduced.

2.4 Fees and Penalties:
- One-Time Processing Fee: 1.5% of the approved loan principal, deducted automatically from the net disbursement amount.
- Prepayment / Early Settlement Penalty: $0. Borrowers may settle the loan in full or make partial principal curtailments at any time without fee or penalty.
- Late Payment Charge: $25 or 5% of the overdue installment (whichever is lower), assessed after a 7-day grace period.

3. REQUIRED VERIFICATION DOCUMENTS
----------------------------------
Applicants must supply:
1. Valid government photo identification (Passport, National ID card, Driver's License).
2. Proof of income: Most recent 2 months of computerized pay slips or consecutive 3 months of bank statements showing regular salary deposits.
3. Proof of residential address: Utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 90 days.

4. EXCEPTION POLICIES & SPECIAL APPROVALS
----------------------------------------
- Near-Prime Exception: Applicants with credit scores between 620 and 649 may qualify if:
  a) The calculated DTI is strictly below 35%; AND
  b) For vehicle loans, a minimum Down Payment (DP) of 20% is provided; AND
  c) Risk surcharge of 2.0% is added to the applicable base APR.

5. MANDATORY COMPLIANCE DISCLOSURE
----------------------------------
Under Federal and Regional Truth in Lending Regulations, every customer must be informed of:
1. Call recording consent at the commencement of the conversation.
2. The specific Annual Percentage Rate (APR) before verbal or digital agreement.
3. Total finance charges and monthly installment schedule.
